Protect the slower pace

Uji, extra viewpoints, and longer temple walks are optional. Airport drivers do not remove hills, steps, or all walking. Request a shorter route where needed.

Recheck against your dates

Confirm museum and market opening days, Nijo Castle palace access, hotel notices, and train baggage rules. Move days around rather than overloading them.
